# Limitations and deviations

The paper does not publish its binary mappings, 14 occupation-group
boundaries, exact regularization coefficient/tuning protocol, seeds, code, or
raw Figure 3 values. The three experiment routes declare their substitutions
and do not silently tune the verifier after observing a result.

Figure coordinates are source-audit evidence, not a new experimental
reproduction. Because an alternate run below 23% does not falsify an
existential "up to" report, the result remains `BLOCKED` rather than being
mislabelled `FALSIFIED`.
