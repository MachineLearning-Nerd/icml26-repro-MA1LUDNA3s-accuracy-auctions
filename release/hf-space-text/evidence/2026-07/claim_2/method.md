# Method

Twenty independent datasets are generated at each of seven sizes (250–16,000). A custom strongly-convex weighted logistic ERM solver exactly matches Equation 4 with fixed lambda and R(theta)=||theta||^2. Candidate payers are identified by the theorem's classification-stability band, then zero-weight refits and 16-step critical-bid searches compute payments. At m=250, three datasets receive all-user leave-one-out checks. 99% t intervals and a tail log-log exponent summarize the expectation.
